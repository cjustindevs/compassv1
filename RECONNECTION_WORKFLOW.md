# Chat reconnection workflow

Helper chat sends a heartbeat every 15 seconds. The minute scheduler detects a heartbeat older than 60 seconds (typically 60?120 seconds after the last heartbeat). Detection notifies active Moderators and the Seeker once. After two minutes from detection the Seeker may keep waiting or request replacement. Moderator Connection review offers an eligible Helper using existing matching. The Helper has two minutes to accept; declines and expired offers return to Moderator review. Returning original Helpers cancel outstanding offers before transfer.

Acceptance rechecks readiness, capacity, supervision, risk and conflicts in a transaction. A linked continuation session isolates the new conversation from the former Helper and preserves the original start time/90-minute deadline. The old session and its messages/referrals remain historical records; original Helpers cannot reclaim the continuation. Seeker navigation follows the continuation automatically. No chat content is copied. Emergency cases notify the responsible Adviser and block ordinary replacement.

Deployment: run `php artisan migrate --force` and keep the Laravel scheduler running. Existing sessions begin monitoring on their first heartbeat after loading updated chat. This detects missing browser heartbeats, not proof of physical network loss: sleeping/background tabs may trigger an interruption. Voice rooms are not monitored by this chat-only implementation. Existing historical reports count session records; a handoff creates a separate continuation record.

Manual verification: use separate Seeker, Helper and Moderator browsers; open active chat; disconnect Helper; wait for detection and grace; request replacement; offer and accept from another eligible Helper. Confirm original deadline, private message isolation, old Helper rejection, reconnect-before-accept cancellation, and no duplicate notifications. Repeat on mobile with screen locking. Browser/network behavior has not been manually verified here.
